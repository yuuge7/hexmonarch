# HexMonarch field manual

How to play, what to do first, and what everything on screen means.

## The game in one minute

You build a criminal empire on the real map. You **plant turfs** on the spot where you are standing, each one earns money every hour, even while the phone is off. Some turfs become **Anchor Hubs** that protect the turfs around them. Hubs in different towns are linked with **relays**, and the more towns you link, the more everything earns. Rival crews hold red turfs, raid yours, and push back harder as you grow. There is no end: every number keeps scaling.

## Your first 15 minutes

Do these in order.

- **1. Plant your first turf.** Open the game where you are right now. The big green button says "Plant turf here". Press it. Cost: 60 credits.
- **2. Make it an Anchor Hub.** With that turf on screen, press "Make this an anchor hub" (300 credits, 120 materials). A hub supplies every turf within 2.5 km, which is most of a town. Supplied turfs earn full income and repair themselves.
- **3. Spend your energy.** Go to **Crew > Jobs** and run jobs until energy is empty. Jobs are your main income for the first days. You are standing in your own turf, so they pay x1.5.
- **4. Plant around you.** You do not have to stand on the exact spot. Tap any open ground inside the dotted green ring, then press "Plant turf there". Turfs must sit 100 m apart, so the dimmed patches around existing turfs are off limits. Stay inside the dashed amber circle (your hub's supply sphere) so the new turfs are protected.
- **5. Buy your first perk.** In **Crew > Perks**, buy **Deep Bench** for more job energy, or **Long Arm** to plant even farther out.
- **6. Turn on auto-plant.** In **Network > Field ops**, set "Auto-plant while travelling" to **Stations**, and switch on **Patrol mode**. Now every station you pass on a train or bus gets a turf, even with the phone in your pocket.

After that the daily loop is: spend energy on jobs, collect what the Event Deck offers nearby, plant where you travel, upgrade your hub.

## Reading the map

- **White dot**: you.
- **Dashed green ring around you**: a new turf would land here. No ring means you are too close to another turf.
- **Wide dotted green ring around you**: your plant range. Tap open ground inside it to plant there.
- **Dimmed patches inside that ring**: too close to an existing turf, nothing can be planted there.
- **Green circle**: your turf, supplied by a hub.
- **Dashed green circle**: your turf with no hub in reach.
- **Amber circle**: your Anchor Hub. The big dashed amber circle around it is its supply sphere.
- **Inner ring inside a turf**: it is a station turf.
- **Red circle**: rival turf.
- **Amber dashed line with moving dots**: a relay between two hubs.
- **Spinning amber diamond**: a convoy. **Blue diamond**: a dead drop. The dotted ring around them is how close you must get.
- **Red target on a hub**: an offensive is coming.
- **Red dashed outline over a large area**: that district is locked down.

Tap a turf to inspect it. Tap the X in the console header to go back to your own position.

Three buttons float on the map, bottom right:

- **Sun**: switches to a white map with dark markings. Use it outside when the sun washes out the dark map. Tap again to go back.
- **Frame**: shows your whole empire.
- **Target**: re-centers on you and follows you.

## Map only, and the turf list

- **Map only**: the arrow at the right of the console header folds the console away, so the main screen is just the map. Tapping the tab that is already open does the same. Tap any tab, the arrow or the header strip to bring the console back. While it is folded, tapping a turf only shows its name in the strip. The game remembers the choice.
- **Turf list**: the list icon in the console header (also **Network > All my turfs**) shows every turf you hold with its income, integrity and distance. Sort by nearest, name, yield or weakest. Filter to hubs, stations, turfs with no hub, or turfs rivals took from you. Tap a row to jump to that turf on the map.

## The five tabs

- **Turf**: the spot you stand on, or the turf you tapped. Plant, upgrade, breach, rename.
- **Network**: income per hour, your hubs and relays, rival pressure, settings, this manual, save export.
- **Crew**: Jobs, Perks, Modules.
- **Ops**: the Event Deck (things to go and do) and the feed of what happened.
- **Vault**: the long game. Network Liquidation and permanent upgrades.

The strip at the top shows credits (CR), materials (MAT), intel (INT), energy (NRG), cryptokeys, and your level.

## Turfs

- You can plant anywhere you physically are. No need to connect to your other turfs.
- New turfs must be 100 m from any other turf, yours or a rival's. The **Close Quarters** perk shrinks that.
- Your plant range is 250 m, and the 100 m no-plant gap does not count against it: you can plant up to 350 m from where you stand, so even standing on a turf you have a full 250 m of usable ground. The **Long Arm** perk adds more.
- The price rises a little with every turf you own.
- **Garrison** upgrades raise a turf's income, defense and size.
- A turf is named after the nearest station, landmark or street. Tap the pencil to rename it.
- Getting rid of a turf: open it and scroll down. **Hold to abandon** removes it, but a rival crew that used to live there moves back in, and auto-plant may plant it again when you pass. **Hold to delete** removes it for good: no crew returns and auto-plant skips the spot until you plant there by hand. Both send the modules to your stash; neither gives a refund.
- Each neighbourhood has a type: **Commercial Transit** (credits), **Municipal Records** (intel), **Industrial Yards** (materials). About one block in seven hides an **anomaly** such as a Bunker (+50% defense) or a Black Market (+50% credits, more raids).

## Stations are special

A turf planted within 250 m of a train, metro or tram station (150 m for bus stations and ferry terminals) becomes a **station turf**:

- +30% credits.
- It needs no hub: it keeps 70% output and never decays on its own.
- As a hub, its relay range is x1.5.
- Only one turf per station gets the bonus. Further turfs near it are ordinary ones, named after their street.

This is why a turf at every station on your line is the backbone of the game.

## Hubs and supply

- Any turf can become a hub. The first costs 300 credits and 120 materials, each later one costs 1.4x more (the tenth is about 6,200 credits).
- A hub supplies every turf of yours inside its sphere: full income, self-repair, and a share of the hub's defense.
- The sphere is 2.5 km at level 1, 3 km at level 4, 3.5 km at level 9. One hub near the middle of a town like Târgoviște holds a turf in every neighbourhood, so spread your turfs out instead of packing them around the hub.
- A normal turf with no hub earns 40% and wears down to 30% integrity, which makes it easy prey for raids. It does not vanish by itself.
- Upgrade a hub to widen its sphere, lengthen its relay range, add module sockets and raise defense.
- Good hub spots: a station in the middle of an area you walk a lot. A big city like Bucharest needs several hubs.

## Districts, relays and trade

- The map is divided into districts roughly the size of a town (about 17 km across). Târgoviște, Pitești, Titu, Găești are each their own district; Bucharest covers two or three.
- A relay links one hub to another. Each hub has one outgoing relay. Range is 10 km + 2.5 km per hub level, x1.5 from a station hub: a level 1 hub reaches 12.5 km (shown as 13 km), a level 1 station hub 19 km, a level 5 station hub 34 km.
- **Stuck at 13 km?** Two ways to reach farther. Upgrade the hub the relay starts from. Or buy the **Signal Boost** perk (Crew > Perks): +20% relay range per rank on every hub, no limit. Rank 3 turns 12.5 km into 20 km; rank 5 doubles it. The relay screen shows the hub's current range at the top.
- All linked hubs form a network. Every extra district in a network adds +30% income to every turf that network supplies; every relay adds +4%.
- So one hub in Bucharest linked to one in Titu and one in Târgoviște earns far more than three hubs in the same town. Long distances need higher hub levels or a chain of hubs along the railway (Gara de Nord, Chitila, Titu, Găești, Pitești).

## Jobs and energy

- Energy refills by 15 per hour up to 30. Full energy is wasted energy.
- Each job costs energy and pays credits, XP and sometimes intel or materials. Bigger jobs unlock as you level.
- Stand inside one of your own turfs for x1.5 pay.
- Every 10 runs of the same job raises its mastery: +5% pay, forever.
- Every run has a small chance to drop a module.
- Jobs matter most early. Later your turfs out-earn them, but jobs stay the fastest XP.

## Perks

Bought in **Crew > Perks** with credits and intel. Perks are permanent, they even survive a Network Liquidation.

- **Long Arm**: plant farther out. Everyone starts with 250 m of reach; rank 1 makes it 330 m, rank 2 is 390 m, rank 5 is 540 m, and it keeps growing. The dotted ring around you shows the range.
- **Deep Reach**: breach rivals and grab convoys and drops from farther away.
- **Close Quarters**: turfs can sit closer together (from 100 m down to 60 m).
- **Signal Boost**: +20% relay range per rank on every hub. This is how you link towns that are out of range.
- **Muscle**: +8% strike, +4% defense per rank.
- **Deep Bench**: +10 maximum energy per rank.
- **Second Wind**: +10% energy regeneration per rank.
- **Street Smarts**: +5% XP per rank.
- **Safehouses**: hub-less turfs hold more integrity (30% up to 90%).

What to buy first: Deep Bench and Second Wind (more jobs per day), then Long Arm, then Signal Boost once your second town is out of relay range, then Muscle when rivals start winning.

## Rivals

- Red turfs belong to three rival factions. Walk into one (you must be inside its circle) and press **Breach**. The button shows your odds. Win and the turf is yours; you often loot a module.
- Rivals **raid** your turfs every hour. A raid that beats the turf's defense damages it; at 0% integrity they take it. Turfs without a hub are hit more often: station turfs twice as often, ordinary ones four times.
- If they take a turf, its hub level and modules stay on it. Go back and breach it to get everything back.
- **Rival pressure** (Network tab) fills up while things are quiet. When it is full, a faction announces an **offensive** on one of your hubs. You have 10 hours to press **Fortify** in the Ops tab.
- Rival strength follows the level of your best hub. They never fall behind.

## The Event Deck

Every 24 hours the game deals 2 to 4 cards. Find them in **Ops**.

- **Lockdown**: one district's income is frozen until you travel there and pay it off. Lifts by itself after 7 days. Only dealt once you hold 5 turfs, and never more than one district in four at a time.
- **Convoy**: appears near one of your hubs for 4 hours. Get within 150 m and intercept it for blueprints, credits and a rare module.
- **Dead drop**: within a kilometre of where you last were, for 24 hours. Walk to it.
- **Surge**: one turf type produces double for 12 hours.
- **Market**: credit income x1.5 or x0.6 for 24 hours.
- **Offensive**: see Rivals. Only from level 5.

## Modules

- Loot with random stats. Socket them into a turf: tap your turf, then an empty socket.
- A module on a hub also boosts every turf the hub supplies.
- Scrap what you do not need for materials.
- **Blueprints** (from convoys) can be decrypted in Crew > Modules into a guaranteed Rare or better module.
- Rarities: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic. There is no best module, a stronger roll always exists.

## Where to spend

- **Credits**: first hub, then turfs, then hub levels. Keep enough back for the next plant when you travel.
- **Materials**: hubs and garrisons eat them. Industrial turfs and the "Boost a delivery van" job supply them.
- **Intel**: perks and breaches. Municipal turfs supply it.

## The long game

- **Tiers** change the story, not the screen. Level 20: the Corporate Syndicate. Level 50: the Sci-Fi Escalation. After that a new Epoch every 50 levels.
- **Network Liquidation** (Vault tab) sells the whole empire for **Offshore Cryptokeys**. It needs 1,000 turfs, a network linking 3 regions (areas about 45 km across, think Bucharest, Târgoviște, Pitești), and a level 30 hub. You lose turfs, modules and resources; you keep cryptokeys, vault upgrades and perks. Cryptokeys buy permanent bonuses with no rank limit.

## Time, offline play and the clock

- The empire keeps running while the app is closed, at 60% income. Raids and events happen too. You get a report when you return.
- The game uses the phone's internal timer, not the date. Changing the date or time earns nothing and gets flagged.

## Changing phones

Your empire lives only on your phone. To move it:

- On the old phone: **Network > Move to another phone > Export save**. Pick where to put the file (Google Drive is the easiest).
- On the new phone: install the game, open it once, then **Network > Move to another phone**, hold the **Import** button and pick that file.

Export a save now and then as a backup too. Importing replaces whatever is on the phone.

## Common questions

- **The plant button is grey.** The spot is within 100 m of another turf (pick a spot outside the dimmed patches), outside your dotted range ring, or you are short on credits or materials (the missing one is shown in red).
- **A relay says "Out of range".** Upgrade the hub it starts from, or buy Signal Boost in Crew > Perks. See Districts, relays and trade.
- **I cannot read the screen in the sun.** Tap the sun button on the map for the white map.
- **The bottom half of the screen is always in the way.** Tap the arrow in the console header for map only.
- **My income says +0/h.** A lockdown has frozen the district. Check Ops.
- **A turf turned red.** A rival took it. Walk in and breach it.
- **Do I have to keep the app open?** No. For auto-plant on a train turn on Patrol mode; it keeps GPS running with a notification.
- **Does it need internet?** Only to download map tiles the first time you see an area. Use "Cache 5 km around me" at home before going offline.
